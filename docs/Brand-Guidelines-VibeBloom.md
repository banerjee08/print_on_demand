## Part 1: Guide for the Human Brand Manager, Designers & Merchandisers

### 1.1 Brand Essence, Positioning & Persona
* **Core Essence:** Sun-drenched wanderlust meets effortless everyday celebration. LouLou & Luxe feels like the golden hour of a summer road trip with your best friends—flirty, empowering, inclusive (sizes US 2–18 / EU 32–50), and never taking itself too seriously.
* **Emotional Hook:** Customers don't just buy a dress, hoodie, or iced-coffee sipper glass—they buy the outfit for their *next memory* (a desert music festival, a beach weekend, a bachelorette trip, a cozy Sunday reset).

---

### 1.2 Refined Color Architecture & WCAG Contrast Pairing Rules

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Critical Web UX Refinement over Raw Palette:**  
> In the raw palette, **Soft Peach (`#FFCCCB`)** and **Mint Flash (`#98FB98`)** have low contrast against white (`1.44:1` and `1.28:1`), which would make buttons and text illegible on mobile screens and fail WCAG AA accessibility.  
> **The Rule:** Use **True Navy (`#001F3F`)** as your universal high-contrast ink and primary button anchor, and use **Soft Peach, Lavender Haze, and Mint Flash** as rich surface backgrounds, badges, and section fills with `#001F3F` text on top!

| Role | Color Name | Hex Code | CSS HSL Token | Approved Text / Foreground Pairing | Contrast Ratio (WCAG) | Primary Storefront Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core Anchor & Ink** | **True Navy** | `#001F3F` | `210 100% 12%` | `#FFFFFF` (White) or `#FFCCCB` (Soft Peach) | **16.4 : 1 (AAA)** | Primary text, sticky header, "Add to Cart" button, footer, logo script. |
| **Core Brand Canvas** | **Soft Peach** | `#FFCCCB` | `1 100% 90%` | `#001F3F` (True Navy) — *Never white text!* | **10.8 : 1 (AAA)** | Hero split background, promo banners, seasonal reversible tag highlight. |
| **Page Background** | **Warm Petal White** | `#FFF9F8` | `9 100% 99%` | `#001F3F` (True Navy) | **15.8 : 1 (AAA)** | Main page canvas (warmer than stark `#FFFFFF` so apparel mockups pop). |
| **Energy & CTA Accent** | **Tangerine** | `#FF8C00` | `33 100% 50%` | `#001F3F` (True Navy) | **7.4 : 1 (AAA)** | "Limited Drop" badges, hover states, cart progress bar, urgency timers. |
| **Dreamy Surface** | **Lavender Haze** | `#E6E6FA` | `240 67% 94%` | `#001F3F` (True Navy) | **12.6 : 1 (AAA)** | AI Personalization Studio cards, "Complete the Vibe" Bundle builder box. |
| **Trust & Stock Pill** | **Mint Flash** | `#98FB98` | `120 93% 79%` | `#001F3F` (True Navy) | **11.9 : 1 (AAA)** | "In Stock Locally", "Free Shipping Unlocked", "100% Print Guarantee" pills. |

### 1.3 Typography System & Conversion Hierarchy (Self-Hosted Google Fonts)

To comply with EU GDPR privacy laws and maximize mobile readability, all three Google Fonts are hosted locally on the storefront server:

1. **Headlines & Emotional Hooks — `Dancing Script` (Weights: `600`, `700`):**
   - **Where to Use:** Hero banners (`48px–72px`), Collection titles, signature `"Dance through whatever comes next"` sign-offs, and script monogram previews.
   - **Strict Guardrail:** **Never** set `Dancing Script` in ALL CAPS, never use it below `24px`, and never use it for product prices, size charts, or checkout forms.
2. **Body, Navigation & Checkout UI — `Nunito` (Weights: `400`, `600`, `700`, `800`):**
   - **Where to Use:** Product titles (`18px–22px` Bold), prices, navigation menus, size/color selectors, product descriptions (`16px` Regular, `1.6` line-height), and checkout inputs.
3. **Playful Badges & Slogan Accents — `Fredoka` (`Fredoka One`, Weight: `600`/`700`):**
   - **Where to Use:** Festival drop badges (`"DESERT BLOOM '26"`), discount pills (`"BUNDLE & SAVE 15%"`), and the **2-inch arc skirt-hem slogan customizer**.

### 1.4 Product Architecture & Merchandising Rules (20+ Printify Categories)

#### Signature Apparel Details
* **Logo Placement:** `"VB"` floral wreath script at `1.5"` on skirt hem, sleeve, or custom inside neck label; seasonal reversible colorways (`True Navy on Soft Peach` for Spring/Summer; `Soft Peach on True Navy` for Fall/Winter).
* **The 60 / 30 / 10 Catalog Rule:**
  1. **60% Whimsical Pattern Play (Hero Apparel):**
     - **Midi Dresses** (All-over print + reversible option), **Sundresses** (2" arc skirt-hem slogan + adjustable straps), **Shirt Dresses** (Collar `"VB"` wreath logo + roll-tab sleeves), **Skirts**, **Swimwear**, **Athleisure & Sportswear**.
  2. **30% Everyday Vibe Staples & Lifestyle Accessories:**
     - **Cozy Layers:** Hoodies, Sweatshirts, T-Shirts, Long Sleeves, Tank Tops, Outerwear, Bottoms.
     - **Drinkware & Home:** Iced-Coffee **Sipper Glass with Straw**, **Mason Jars**, **Engraved Tumblers & Water Bottles**, **Scented Candles**.
     - **Carry & Tech:** **Embroidered Hats**, **Tote Bags & Weekenders**, **Phone Cases**, **Socks**, **Tech, Travel & Stationery Accessories**.
  3. **10% Quarterly Niche Drops & Personalized Capsules (5–7 Variants/Quarter):**
     - **Custom Personalization:** 2" curved hem slogans, custom embroidered initials on hoodies/hats, laser-engraved names on tumblers, custom bachelorette/wedding-guest party bundles.

> [!WARNING]
> **Trademark-Safe Merchandising Rule (Critical for Printify, Meta, TikTok & PayPal Compliance):**

> * ❌ *"Coachella '26"* $\to$ ✅ **"Desert Bloom '26" / "Indio Sunset Festival Edit"**

## Part 3: Regional Storefront Strategy — Global Brand Shell vs. Regional Model Casting & Prints

When operating regional storefronts under our **Single-Backend Root + Subfolder Architecture** (**Phase 1: `vibebloom.com` for USA + Canada** $\to$ **Phase 2: `vibebloom.com/uk/` for UK + Ireland** $\to$ **Phase 3: `vibebloom.com/eu/` for Continental Europe** $\to$ **Phase 4: `vibebloom.com/au/` for Australia + NZ**), we follow the **"85% Global Shell / 15% Regional Cultural Chameleon"** rule:

### 3.1 Keep 100% Identical Across All Regions (The 85% Global Brand Shell)
* **Why:** Preserves global brand equity, prevents duplicate theme/CSS maintenance, and ensures 100% shared browser font/stylesheet caching across subfolders.
* **What Stays Fixed:**
  1. **Storefront UI Color Palette & Theme Presets:** Warm Alabaster (`#FAF8F5`), Pure Surface (`#FFFFFF`), Espresso Ink (`#18181B`), Botanical Rose (`#9E4758`), Soft Peach (`#FFCCCB`), True Navy (`#001F3F`), and Midnight Atelier Dark Mode (`#0C0D10`).
  2. **Typography Hierarchy:** `Cormorant Garamond` + `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Storefront UI) and `Dancing Script` + `Nunito` + `Fredoka` (Garment & Customizer Fonts).
  3. **3:4 Lookbook Card Architecture & Universal Checkout Safeguards:** Inline Cart Remove Undo + `vibebloom_removed_cart_cache` (`COMEBACK15`), Dynamic `Apply Code` $\leftrightarrow$ `Remove Code` toggle, and Universal Step-2 Receiver Address Verification across all payment rails.

### 3.2 Customize 100% Per Region Cluster (The 15% Model Casting, Print & Festival Layer)
* **Why:** Shoppers convert at **35%–55% higher rates** when models, environments, floral species, spelling, and seasonal weather match their local culture.
* **What Changes Per Subfolder Storefront (Generated in `Banana POD`):**
  1. **Model Selection & Social Media / Catalog Lookbooks:**
     - **USA + Canada (`vibebloom.com` — Phase 1):** Diverse North American model casting (Caucasian, Black, Latina, Asian-American/Canadian women) shot in sunlit California/Austin patios, New England coastal porches, Pacific Northwest autumn trails, and Toronto cafes.
     - **UK + Ireland (`vibebloom.com/uk/`):** British & Irish model casting in Cotswolds stone cottage gardens, London mews streets, and Dublin Georgian townhouses.
     - **Continental Europe (`vibebloom.com/eu/`):** Parisian, Milanese, Berlin, and Nordic editorial lookbook casting in Haussmann balconies and Mediterranean courtyards.
     - **Australia + NZ (`vibebloom.com/au/`):** Sun-drenched Sydney/Byron Bay coastal and botanical garden casting (matching inverted Southern Hemisphere seasons).
  2. **Garment Print Motifs, Spelling & Sizing:**
     - **USA + Canada:** Peony, Sunflower, California Poppy, Wildflower, Autumn Maple leaf; *"Mom / Mama"*, *"Sweater"*, *"Color"*, *"Favorite"*; US/CA sizes (`XS–2XL`), `oz` (`40oz Tumbler`).
     - **UK + Ireland:** English Rose, Bluebell, Foxglove, Hydrangea, Lavender; *"Mum"*, *"Jumper"*, *"Colour"*, *"Favourite"*, *"Mothering Sunday (March)"*; UK sizes (`UK 6–20`).
     - **Continental Europe:** Fine-line herbarium illustrations, Latin/French botanical taxonomy (*Rosa Centifolia*), zero heavy English slang; EU sizes (`EU 34–48`).
